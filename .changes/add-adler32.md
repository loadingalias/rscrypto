Add Adler-32 from RFC 1950, the checksum of zlib streams and HTTP `deflate`, behind the new `adler32` leaf feature, part of `checksums`. `Adler32` implements `Checksum`: one-shot `checksum`, incremental `update`, `finalize`, `reset` to the standard initial value, and `with_initial` to resume from an earlier checksum. It implements `ChecksumCombine` with zlib's `adler32_combine` semantics for every length. `Adler32::resume` is the const form of `with_initial`, under `std` the state implements `std::io::Write`, and with `alloc` `Adler32::buffered()` and `checksum::buffered::BufferedAdler32` coalesce short updates as the CRC wrappers do. `checksum::config::Adler32Force` and `Adler32Config`, with `RSCRYPTO_ADLER32_FORCE`, force a kernel for testing as the CRC force controls do. It needs neither `alloc` nor `std` and keeps no input. AArch64 uses NEON kernels, with the dot-product extension where the CPU has it; other targets use the portable kernel. It is non-cryptographic: it detects accidental corruption and gives no authentication. CRC outputs are unchanged.
